After the exam

Results appear in the assessment overview within minutes of students uploading their answer files.

  1. Assessment overviewDistribution and overall performance.
  2. Item analysisFind questions the class missed or where distractors did not work. Candidates for scoring adjustments or retirement.
  3. Category performancePerformance by topic across the cohort.

Adjusting scores

After reviewing item analysis, you may need to adjust scoring on individual questions. Common adjustments include granting full credit on a flawed question or accepting an additional correct answer.

Watch out

Coordinate scoring changes with OME

Email OME before making adjustments so your changes and any adjustments OME has queued do not conflict.

Releasing results to students

Once scoring adjustments are final, OME releases the results. Students receive an email notification and can view their score in the Examplify app or the student portal.

Note

Exam review follows the setting chosen before posting

If the assessment was set to Immediate review, students already saw their answers after uploading. If Delayed, students see them after the date you configured. If None, students see only their score.
