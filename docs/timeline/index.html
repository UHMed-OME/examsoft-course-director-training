Exam timeline

  1. 7 business days before Draft handed off to OME You

    Questions written, ordered, and categorized in the assessment. You have told OME which exam review setting you want.

  2. 7 to 2 business days before Proofing OME

    OME previews on an iPad, flags issues, prints backup copies, and builds the proctor deck.

  3. 36 to 48 hours before Posted OME

    Students can now download the exam onto their iPads.

  4. Exam day Password announced Proctor

    Examplify locks the iPad for the duration.

  5. After upload Results visible You

    The assessment overview updates as answer files come in.

Watch out

Fix content before the download window opens

Once a student has downloaded the assessment, questions and answer keys lock. Errors after that point become scoring adjustments.
