Building and posting

JABSOM convention

Start from last year's exam

Most JABSOM assessments are built by duplicating the previous year's exam. That keeps question order, categorization, and scoring setup intact.

Duplicate vs. repost

DuplicateRepost
New Exam IDYesNo
Can change questionsYesNo
ReportingIts own reportsCombines with original
Use forNext year's versionMake-up sitting

Working order

  1. Duplicate last year's assessmentName it with the academic year.
  2. Remove retired questionsItems the course no longer teaches or that performed badly.
  3. Add and tag new questionsEvery new question needs at least one category.
  4. Check order and scoringConfirm point values and partial credit settings.
  5. Add attachmentsStay under the 200 MB ceiling.
  6. Hand off to OME7 business days before the exam.

Text highlighting

Students can highlight and strikethrough text in Examplify. This is on by default but can be turned off per assessment under Options.

JABSOM convention

Leave text highlighting on

JABSOM keeps highlighting enabled for all exams.

Exam review settings

Exam review controls whether students can see the questions and their answers after submitting. You configure this before posting under the Exam Review tab.

SettingWhat students seeWhen to use
ImmediateQuestions and answers right after uploadingFormative quizzes where fast feedback matters
DelayedQuestions and answers after a date you setSummative exams where you need time to review item analysis first
NoneScore only, no question reviewWhen the item bank must stay secure
Critical

Choose the review setting before posting

You cannot change the exam review setting after the assessment is posted. Decide up front whether students will see their answers immediately, on a delayed date, or not at all.

iPad preview

After posting, the assessment takes 5 to 10 minutes to appear in the Examplify app on iPads. Plan accordingly when testing before exam day.
