Building and handing off

JABSOM convention

Start from last year's exam

Most JABSOM assessments are built by duplicating the previous year's exam. That keeps question order, categorization, and scoring setup intact.

Duplicate vs. repost

DuplicateRepost
New Exam IDYesNo
Can change questionsYesNo
ReportingIts own reportsCombines with original
Use forNext year's versionMake-up sitting

Working order

  1. Duplicate last year's assessmentName it with the academic year.
  2. Remove retired questionsItems the course no longer teaches or that performed badly.
  3. Add and tag new questionsEvery new question needs at least one category.
  4. Check order and scoringConfirm point values and partial credit settings.
  5. Add attachmentsStay under the 200 MB ceiling.
  6. Hand off to OME7 business days before the exam. Use the checklist below.

Exam review setting

Exam review controls whether students see the questions and their answers after submitting. You choose the setting. OME applies it when posting the exam.

SettingWhat students seeWhen to use
ImmediateQuestions and answers right after uploadingFormative quizzes where fast feedback matters
DelayedQuestions and answers after a date you setSummative exams where you need time to review item analysis first
NoneScore only, no question reviewWhen the item bank must stay secure
Critical

Decide the review setting before handoff

The setting cannot be changed after the exam is posted. Tell OME your choice when you hand off the draft.

Handoff checklist

Before you hand off, confirm each of these:

  • Every question has at least one category.
  • Question order and vignette groups are in the sequence you want.
  • Point values and partial credit settings are confirmed.
  • Images students need on the printed backup are embedded, not attached.
  • Attachments total under 200 MB.
  • You have picked an exam review setting: Immediate, Delayed (with a date), or None.

Then tell OME the draft is ready and which review setting you chose. OME posts the exam. Do not post it yourself unless OME asks.
