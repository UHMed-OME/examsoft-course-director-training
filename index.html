ExamSoft is where JABSOM writes exam questions, assembles them into an assessment, delivers it on student iPads, and reports on performance. You will spend nearly all your time in two places: the question bank, and the assessment you build from it. Writing a question does not put it on an exam. Those are separate steps.

Critical

Your draft is due 7 business days before the exam

Every other date is scheduled backwards from that one. If you remember nothing else on this page, remember this.

Do these three things this week

  1. Check that you can sign in Go to the portal. Institution ID is hawaiimed. It uses your UH login, so there is no separate ExamSoft password. If it refuses you, see Account and sign-in.
  2. Set your fonts to Arial 12 My Preferences, Question Creation tab, two font fields, then Save. Do it before you write anything or you will be reformatting later. Steps and a screenshot are in Set your preferences first.
  3. Open last year's exam for your course You will almost certainly build this year's version from it rather than starting fresh. See Building an assessment.

How an exam gets delivered

  1. 7 business days before Draft complete You

    Questions written, ordered, categorized, attached. This is the handoff.

  2. 7 to 2 days before Proofing OME staff

    Preview on iPad, corrections back to you, re-preview, print backups, build proctor materials.

  3. 36 to 48 hours before Posted OME staff

    The download window opens so students can pull the exam onto their iPads.

  4. Exam day Password announced Proctor

    Nobody can open the exam before this, even with the file downloaded.

  5. Minutes after upload Results visible You

    The assessment overview shows how the class did.

Watch out

Three things that surprise new course directors

Every question needs at least one category tag, or it disappears from reporting. Drag and Drop does not score the order answers are dragged in. And once the exam is posted and one student has downloaded it, the questions lock.
