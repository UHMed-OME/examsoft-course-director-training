ExamSoft is where JABSOM writes exam questions, puts them into an assessment, delivers that assessment on student iPads, and reports on how the class did. You will spend most of your time in two places: the question bank and the assessment you build from it. Writing a question does not put it on an exam. Those are separate steps.

Critical

Your draft is due 7 business days before the exam

Every other date is scheduled backwards from that one. If you remember one thing from this page, make it this.

Do these three things this week

  1. Sign in to the portal Go to the Legacy portal. The institution ID is hawaiimed. It uses your UH login, so there is no separate ExamSoft password. If it will not let you in, see Account and sign-in.
  2. Set your fonts to Arial 12 Open My Preferences, go to the Question Creation tab, set both font fields to Arial 12, then Save. Do this before you write anything. The screenshot below shows what the page looks like.
  3. Open last year's exam for your course You will almost certainly build from it rather than starting fresh. See Building an assessment.

How an exam gets delivered

  1. 7 business days before Draft complete You

    Questions written, ordered, categorized, attached. This is the handoff.

  2. 7 to 2 days before Proofing OME staff

    Staff preview on an iPad, send corrections back to you, re-preview, print backups, build proctor materials.

  3. 36 to 48 hours before Posted OME staff

    The download window opens so students can pull the exam onto their iPads.

  4. Exam day Password announced Proctor

    Nobody can open the exam until then, even with the file downloaded.

  5. Minutes after upload Results visible You

    The assessment overview shows how the class did.

Watch out

Three things that surprise new course directors

Every question needs at least one category tag, or it disappears from reporting. Drag and Drop does not score the order answers are dragged in. And once the exam is posted and one student has downloaded it, the questions lock.
