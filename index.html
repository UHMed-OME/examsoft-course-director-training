ExamSoft Course Director Training Guide

Getting started

First-time setup for new course directors.

  1. Sign in to the portalGo to www.examsoft.com/hawaiimed. Use the Administrators / Faculty login at the bottom of the page, not the student login at the top.
  2. Set both fonts to Arial 12Open My Preferences from the gear icon, go to the Question Creation tab, set both font fields to Arial 12, then Save.
  3. Open last year's examFind your course's most recent assessment and open it. You will build this year's version from it.
Critical

Your draft is due 7 business days before the exam

OME needs the full seven days to proof the assessment, preview it on an iPad, print backup copies, and build proctor materials.
