First-time setup

  1. Sign in to the portal. Go to www.examsoft.com/hawaiimed. Use your UH credentials. If you get "No associated ExamSoft account found," email Jesse Thompson (jessetho@hawaii.edu) to get your External ID corrected. An incognito window will not fix it.
  2. Set both fonts to Arial 12. Open My Preferences from the gear icon, go to the Question Creation tab, set both font fields to Arial 12, then Save. JABSOM standardizes on this.
  3. Open last year's exam. Find your course's most recent assessment and open it. You will build this year's version from it.

Questions

Questions live in the question bank. Get there from the Questions menu or Create Questions on the portal home.

Tag every question with at least one category

An untagged question is invisible to category reporting. Use the Add or Remove Category button while writing, or bulk-tag from the questions table.

Apply tags, but leave the tree alone

The category tree is shared across all courses and about nine years of data. Renaming or moving a node changes what past reports mean. If your course needs a new category, ask OME to add it.

Question groups (vignettes)

A question group lets you share a single clinical vignette or stimulus across several related questions. The vignette stays visible as students work through each question in the group.

Hot Spot

  • Pick rectangle or polygon first. Polygon is better for irregular structures.
  • Drag the image onto the question. GIF, JPG, and PNG all work.
  • Drag to outline the correct region. Students can pinch to zoom on the iPad.

Drag and Drop

Drag and Drop does not score sequence

Order makes no difference to the score. If you need sequence scored, use Ordering instead.

Partial credit subtracts points

Students gain points for correct answers dragged in and lose the same for wrong ones (floor is 0). Warn them in the stem.

Attachments and media

An assessment-level attachment stays available for the whole exam (one file only, good for a formulary or reference sheet). A question-level attachment only opens on that question.

200 MB total per assessment

That ceiling covers every attachment and inline image in the assessment combined.

Building assessments

Start from last year's exam

Most JABSOM assessments are built by duplicating the previous year's exam. That keeps question order, categorization, and scoring setup intact.

Working order

  1. Duplicate last year's assessment. Name it with the academic year.
  2. Remove retired questions. Items the course no longer teaches or that performed badly.
  3. Add and tag new questions. Every new question needs at least one category.
  4. Check order and scoring. Confirm point values and partial credit settings.
  5. Add attachments. Stay under the 200 MB ceiling.
  6. Hand off to OME. 7 business days before the exam.

Text highlighting

ExamSoft includes a text highlighting toggle in the assessment settings. When enabled, students can highlight and strikethrough answer choices during the exam. Enable or disable it before posting.

Exam review settings

Exam review controls whether students can review their answers after submitting. You must choose the review setting before the exam is posted, because it cannot be changed afterward.

SettingWhen students see review
ImmediateRight after submitting their exam
DelayedAfter OME enables it (gives time for scoring adjustments first)

Posting

OME posts your exam

Do not post your own exam unless OME has asked you to.

Max Downloads is always 1

A standing JABSOM rule.

After OME posts the assessment, allow 5 to 10 minutes for the exam to appear in the iPad Examplify app. OME previews the exam on an iPad before posting to catch layout issues.

Fix content before the download window opens

Once a student has downloaded the assessment, questions and answer keys lock. Errors after that point become scoring adjustments.

Your draft is due 7 business days before the exam

OME needs the full seven days to proof the assessment, preview it on an iPad, print backup copies, and build proctor materials.

Exam day

Students sit the exam in Examplify on iPad. Once the exam starts, the app locks the device. Screenshots, copy-paste, and internet access all stop until the exam ends.

Lockdown and resume codes

If a student gets locked out of Examplify during an exam (usually from a button combination), they need a resume code to get back in. The proctor has it on the printed materials OME prepares. The exam password is announced in the room by the proctor. It never goes in an email.

Hard copy backups

OME prints backup copies during the proofing window. These are available in the exam room in case of a device failure that cannot be resolved with a resume code.

After the exam

Results appear in the assessment overview within minutes of students uploading their answer files.

Review your results

  1. Assessment overview. Distribution and overall performance.
  2. Item analysis. Find questions the class missed or where distractors did not work. These are candidates for scoring adjustments or retirement.
  3. Category performance. Performance by topic across the cohort.

Adjusting scores

Talk to OME before adjusting scores. Accepting a second correct answer or dropping an item changes the record for every student. OME coordinates the adjustment to keep reporting consistent.

Quick reference FAQ

I can't sign in. What do I do?
You probably see "No associated ExamSoft account found." The External ID on your account needs to be corrected. Email Jesse Thompson at jessetho@hawaii.edu. An incognito window will not fix it.
When is my draft due?
7 business days before the exam. OME needs the full week to proof the exam on an iPad, print backup copies, and prepare proctor materials.
What happens if my draft is late?
A late draft compresses proofing, not the exam date. The proofing steps run in sequence and need the full 7 days.
Do I have to tag every question?
Yes. At least one category per question. Untagged questions are invisible to category reporting.
How does partial credit work?
Partial Credit caps how many choices a student can pick. +/- Partial Credit adds points for correct picks and subtracts for wrong ones. Mention it in the stem so students know.
Can I edit questions after the exam is posted?
Not once a student has downloaded it. Errors after that point become post-exam scoring adjustments.
Who posts my exam?
OME posts it. Do not post your own exam unless OME has asked you to.
Can students open the exam before the proctor gives the password?
No. The exam stays sealed until the proctor announces the password in the room. The password never goes in an email.
What is a resume code?
If a student gets locked out of Examplify during an exam (usually from a button combination), they need a resume code to get back in. The proctor has it on the printed materials OME prepares.
Can I adjust scores on my own?
Talk to OME first. Accepting a second correct answer or dropping an item changes the record for every student.
